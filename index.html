How it works

  1. KickoffWe map why your best clients bought and turn that into targeting rules.
  2. Safe setupSeparate sending domains (never your main one), authenticated and warmed for 2–4 weeks.
  3. ResearchAI builds a verified list of accounts with a current buying trigger: a new leader, hiring, a launch.
  4. Outreach you approveShort, plain-text emails built on each prospect's trigger. Nothing goes out until you approve the copy.
  5. Replies → meetingsA person reviews every reply before anyone answers. Interested prospects book straight into your calendar, and you get a one-page brief.

What's automated, and what isn't

AI does

  • Account research, verification, trigger detection
  • First drafts of every email
  • Scheduling, rotation, out-of-office and bounce handling
  • CRM logging and weekly reports

A human does

  • Defines your ICP and offer with you
  • Approves copy (you do too)
  • Reviews and approves replies to interested prospects
  • Monthly strategy review and optimization

We don't scrape LinkedIn, run bots on your profile, use purchased "guaranteed" lists, or send from your main domain.

FAQ

Will this sound like AI?

Emails are under 80 words, built on a real trigger, and approved by you. If a line sounds generic, it doesn't go out.

Is cold email legal?

Yes, when it's done properly: country-specific rules, a real postal address, a working opt-out, and no harvested lists. We exclude countries where B2B cold email needs prior consent (e.g. Germany).

What if it doesn't work for us?

You'll see it in the weekly numbers. The performance credit applies in months 2–4, and after 90 days you can stop with 30 days' notice.

Who owns the domains and data?

You do. Domains are registered for your benefit and transfer to you if you leave.

Do you do LinkedIn automation or calls?

No automation. On Growth and above we draft LinkedIn messages for you to send yourself. No cold calling.

Do you have case studies?

Not yet for this service. We're onboarding founding clients now and won't show results we haven't produced.

What CRMs do you support?

HubSpot and Pipedrive natively. Others by weekly export.
