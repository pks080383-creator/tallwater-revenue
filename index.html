FAQ

You have no case studies. Why should I trust you?

Because you do not have to take our word for anything. List buyers judge a free 25-contact sample before paying. Home-services owners watch the system handle an enquiry before signing up. Prices and exclusions are on this page.

What does "verified" mean on a lead list?

Each email is checked with a verification tool and labelled with its result. Each row links to the public source for the contact. Where we cannot confirm a field, we say so in the file instead of filling the gap with a guess. You are responsible for lawful use of the data in your market, including GDPR and PECR for EU and UK contacts.

Will the home-services follow-up sound like a robot?

No. Messages are short, plain, and written in your company's voice during setup, and you approve them before launch. One follow-up, one reminder if they do not book. No five-email drips on a burst pipe.

Do you guarantee more jobs or revenue?

No. We install and run the system, and we show you every enquiry and what happened to it. If your enquiries are not going cold, you do not need us. If they are, the Monday report will show it, and you can cancel month to month after the first 30 days.
